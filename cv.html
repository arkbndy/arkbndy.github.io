Research profile

I am a theoretical condensed-matter physicist working on quantum materials: I build materials-relevant theory for quantum geometry, topological and correlated phases, semiconductor and quantum transport, and unconventional responses. My work brings together first-principles and Wannier calculations, symmetry-adapted tight-binding models, quantum transport and many-body methods — and I enjoy it most in close partnership with experimental and synthetic groups, on kagome materials, two-dimensional systems, molecular magnets and complex-oxide interfaces. A substantial part of my work sits at the chemistry–physics boundary: close to half of my papers have appeared in chemistry and materials-chemistry journals, including Chemical Society Reviews, Chemistry of Materials, Dalton Transactions and Journal of Materials Chemistry C.

Research themes: quantum geometry and non-linear Hall transport; topological and flat-band quantum materials; kagome and line-graph lattices; Dirac and semiconducting monolayers; oxide interfaces, altermagnets and spintronics. I am always happy to hear about a new problem or a new collaboration, from theorists and experimentalists alike.
